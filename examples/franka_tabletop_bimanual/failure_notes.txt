1. RIGHT ARM (arm 1) DROPS ITS SAFETY LINK MID-RUN: HARDWARE, OPEN
--------------------------------------------------------------------
What happens
  The control box loses its internal link to the arm. The arm's light goes off
  (or the system becomes "not operational"), FCI refuses connections, and the
  joint stream stops.

When (all on the right arm, all within seconds of a move starting)
  19:12:58  Desk: "A Fieldbus/Connection error has occurred"; while restarting:
            "Joint firmware update failed". A Reboot from Desk cleared it.
  19:40:07  Desk: "A FSoE Connection error has been triggered. The safe connection
            between the Master Controller and the Arm has been interrupted."
            "Acknowledge" failed ("error could not be acknowledged"); a full power
            cycle of both arms cleared it.
  19:54:33  Same signature during a right-arm-only test (soak.py): 90 planned and
            straight moves in 3.4 min, with NO gripper commands. The robot's own
            joint stream went silent about 1 s before any other error.

Why we think it is hardware (arm, its cable, or the control box)
  - FSoE is the control box <-> arm safety bus; FCI traffic from the laptop does
    not travel on it.
  - It reproduces with no gripper commands, and the robot stops sending first.
  - The left arm, with identical software, has not faulted all day.
  - The right arm ran about 2 days without this before both arms were moved to a
    new table this morning (2026-11-22).
  Caveat: all three faults came after the Hand-in-its-own-process change (19:08).
  The soak test without Hand commands still faulted, which makes that change an
  unlikely cause, but its Hand program still polls the Hand's state at 10 Hz.

Already tried
  Cables inspected and reported fine; Desk Reboot (worked once); full power cycle
  of both arms (worked); arm cable reseated.

Next
  - Contact Franka support with the times and messages above, plus the arm and
    control box serials (Desk -> Settings -> System).
  - Optional isolation test: power off, swap which control box drives which arm.
    If the fault follows the arm, it is the arm or its cable; if it stays with the
    box, it is the box. Re-check IPs and Desk setup afterwards.

Recovering after it happens
  1. Stop the dimOS stack (Ctrl+C) so nothing keeps reconnecting.
  2. In Desk: acknowledge the error; if that fails, Shut down, wait about 10 s after
     the fans stop, and power on.
  3. Unlock joints, Execution mode, end effector initialised and powered, FCI on.
  4. Start the stack. Before anything else, lift any arm that stopped low near the
     table straight up (handoff.py's Arms.straight), then plan it to ready.
     Initialising the end effector homes the Hand, so it opens and drops what it held.


2. FIXED TODAY (software): kept here because they explain earlier symptoms
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
a) A leftover dimOS stack held a second Gripper connection to arm 1.
   Symptom: Hand "Net Exception" / UDP timeouts; Desk "End Effector: Not connected".
   Fix: stop stale stacks first (pgrep -af "dimos run").

b) Hand calls froze the whole Python process (pylibfranka holds the GIL for about
   1 s per move or grasp).
   Symptom: "joints were last read 1.3 s ago", then "the control session ended away
   from its target", and the arm connection faulted silently.
   Fix: the Hand runs as its own program (dimos.robot.manipulators.franka.hand_process),
   commits 56617c379 and 3e25dd66a. Measured on hardware: arm reads every 10 ms
   through Hand moves.

c) dimOS reported trajectories as COMPLETED even when the arm never moved (the
   connection was faulted, or the SeparationGuard was holding the arm).
   Symptom: the handoff continued, and the giver opened with nobody holding the
   eraser, which dropped it.
   Fix (handoff.py): every move is checked against the measured joints or TCP;
   any guard refusal or arm fault stops the cycle; the giver opens only after the
   receiver's fingers have visibly closed to about the object's width.
   Still open in dimOS: the trajectory task should not report COMPLETED in these cases.

d) Injecting commands straight onto <side>_position_command interleaved with the
   coordinator's own repeated hold target, and the connection's jump guard refused
   the mix. Commands must go through the coordinator (execute_trajectory or a task).

e) The planner did not know about the table, so plans could dip into it.
   Fix: handoff.py adds a box obstacle with its top 1 cm below the tabletop
   (flush with the top, the bases count as colliding).

f) The pick re-solved the planned grasp's IK and landed on a contorted, colliding
   branch; the planner refused it. Fix: reuse the plan's joints, and reject IK
   solutions that collide or put a hand part at the table.

g) The arm-to-arm calibration had an unexplained 2.7 deg roll and 2 cm rise, which
   made the left camera see the table tilted.
   Fix: planar fit (x, y, yaw), since both bases sit flat on one level table;
   4.3 mm RMS over all board corners.

h) The tripod could not see objects under an arm at ready, and saw cables and the
   calibration boards as objects.
   Fix: the wrist cameras act as a fallback; candidates seen by more cameras win;
   masks for base surroundings (25 cm), the boards, reach, and the table outline.

4. 2026-11-22/23, pen demo (pen_demo.py): what went wrong, and the rules it left
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
a) Planned moves with a pen in the Hand drove it into the table, twice (cartesian_reflex
   on the left arm, ~00:40 and ~00:41). The planner models the arms, the wrist cameras
   and the table, not what a Hand holds. Rule: with something held, only straight moves
   near surfaces; planned moves go between raised poses and every trajectory point is
   checked first (pen_demo.carry), else nothing moves.
b) Restarting the stack while a Hand held the pen: on startup the connection sent the
   Hand "move to its current width" (00:59:53), which holds the width without squeezing.
   A re-grasp from there failed ("libfranka gripper: Command failed!", 01:00:21) and the
   Hand let go: the pen fell ~22 cm. Rule: never restart the stack or re-grasp with
   something held; put it down first.
c) Right arm hardware fault again (~00:53, Desk reboot; earlier 23:01 "Generic joint
   error" needing the self tests). After the right arm's reboot the planner had no joint
   readings for either arm (the right Hand's finger was missing from the joint state)
   until the stack was restarted.

   corners were read at 1x, and the run fell back to a calibration 3.5 cm off. Fixed:
   detection on a 2x enlargement (all 6 corners), the last good pose saved to
