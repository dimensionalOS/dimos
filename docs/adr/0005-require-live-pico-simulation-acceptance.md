# Require live PICO simulation acceptance

Full-body SONIC teleoperation is not complete when synthetic fixtures or recorded frames pass alone. Acceptance requires a physical PICO and Motion Trackers to drive the MuJoCo G1 through the production WebXR browser, coordinator, specialized SONIC task, and policy path. Automated conversion, routing, and replay tests remain required preconditions, but they establish readiness for the live test rather than completion.
