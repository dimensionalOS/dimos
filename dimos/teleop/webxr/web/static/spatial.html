SPATIAL TELEOP
Connecting to the station…

Use either hand: pinch thumb and index finger at a round tool handle to move that arm. Pinch its opening control and slide toward OPEN or CLOSE to adjust the jaws; the other hand can keep moving the arm. Release to hold.

Solid markers show measured opening; the white outline shows your request. Point at the scene, pinch and release to select an observed surface. Surfaces come from RGB-D; arms follow joint feedback.

Controllers: hold the grip to move your corresponding arm, trigger closes its jaws. Release grip to hold. Tool mode uses trigger near handles and sliders. Point and trigger to use menus. Classic hands: index pinch moves; middle pinch closes.

Drag a panel's title bar with pinch or trigger. HUD pins it to your view; Unpin returns it to the workspace. Dragging a pinned panel also unpins it.

Pinch with both hands in free space to grab the workspace. Move, spread or turn your hands; release one to keep dragging, both to park. Turn an open palm upward for “Bring workspace here”, then point and pinch with the other hand.

Connecting to recorder…

These controls work here and in the headset. Home uses each arm's configured home pose and leaves teleop paused. Stop & save keeps the episode; Discard deletes the take.

Object recognition: no detector connected.

Desktop: drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. Motion control uses tracked hands or controllers.
