# reflective_column_node (class Location) vs our Rust port: read-only audit

Verdict: the per-sweep maths is a faithful port. Every constant, comparison type, FMA site, PCL ordering quirk and the
stale-pair quirk in FindTargetRefleColu matches, and `reflective_column_replay` reproduces all 390 logged sweeps. The
differences are in the plumbing around it: what goes in (merged cloud, the driver's range cut), how sweeps queue,
where the output filter runs (in the controller, behind a newest-only mailbox), two unfused/`hypot` arithmetic spots
in the filter, and the vendor topics and service that we do not publish.

Addresses are Ghidra's (image base 0x100000). In `rc_dis.txt` they are 0x100000 lower. Constants were read from the
binary with rdconst.py.

Our files (root `dimos/robot/deeprobotics/m20/controller/rust/`):
- D = src/dock/detector.rs
- M = src/dock/detector_module.rs
- V = src/dock/vendor.rs
- K = src/dock/docking.rs
- P = src/dock/pcl.rs
- L = src/dock/libm.rs
- C = src/module.rs (the controller)

## Differences to fix (by risk)

1. **The start filter and jump gate see a newest-only mailbox, not every sweep.**
   - Vendor (0x163378-0x163518): `FilterPositionData` and the 0.8 m gate run inside the detector on every processed sweep. A NaN position passes both gates and is published.
   - Ours: `ReflectorFilter` runs in the controller's 1 kHz `ingest` (K:1043-1054) on the single newest pose in `shared.dock.pose` (C:435-441). A pose that is overwritten before a tick never enters the 15-sweep window or the jump reference. A non-finite pose is dropped at C:430.
   - Fix: run `ReflectorFilter` in the detector module and publish only what it publishes, or queue every pose to the controller.
   - Test: in a Docking harness, deliver two dock poses between two ticks and assert the window advances by 2. Also run the 2026-11-24 per-sweep poses through module → controller with random 0-2 ms delivery jitter and assert the published sequence equals `ReflectorFilter` run on every sweep.

2. **The input cloud is not the vendor's.**
   - Vendor (0x162640, node_driver config.yaml): /LIDAR/POINTS is the driver's merged front + rear cloud (`send_separately: false`) in base_link, with returns closer than 0.2 m or farther than 60 m already dropped (`min_distance` / `max_distance`, `dense_points`).
   - Ours (M:288, D:224): front frame only, and no 0.2 m / 60 m cut on the detector path. The obstacle path does apply 0.2 m (M:240-255).
   - The replay matched all 390 logged sweeps front-only, so rear returns seem not to reach the box. That is unverified for other headings or docks.
   - Test: put a 250-intensity return 0.15 m from the front lidar inside the box (x ≈ 0.45 m in base_link) in a synthetic sweep and assert it is not counted. Also re-run `reflective_column_replay` on the 2026-11-24 capture with the rear Airy decoded and merged as the driver does, and assert the logged counts still match.

3. **The sweep queue policy.**
   - Vendor (0x1617e0, 0x1638d0): a deque of at most 2. The callback pops the front before pushing when it holds 2 or more, and the thread takes the *older* one, then sleeps 1 ms. Clouds are dropped while closed. Up to 2 clouds queued before a close survive it and are the first samples after the next open (the queue is never cleared in ServiceCallback).
   - Ours (M:274): every sweep is processed in arrival order, and the backlog behaviour of the transport is unverified. After `restart()` only fresh sweeps count.
   - Test: wrap `detect` with an artificial 150 ms delay, feed 10 Hz sweeps, and assert the processed sequence is the vendor's (keep 2, drop oldest, take the older). For close/open, assert that a sweep queued at close time is the first window sample after reopen. Implement this, or document it as a deliberate deviation.

4. **The jump distance uses `hypot`.**
   - Vendor (0x1633f0-0x163424): `sqrt(dx*dx + dy*dy)`, unfused, compared `0.8 < d`.
   - Ours (V:826): `hypot`, which rounds differently in the last bit and can flip a jump exactly at 0.8 m.
   - Test: property test over 1e6 random (dx, dy) asserting our jump equals `(dx*dx + dy*dy).sqrt()` bitwise, after changing V:826 to that.

5. **The start filter's variance is unfused.**
   - Vendor (0x160e84-88): `acc = fmadd(d, d, acc)`.
   - Ours (V:840): `(s - m).powi(2)` then `sum`, unfused. σ can differ in the last bit and flip a sample on the 3σ edge.
   - Test: after switching to `fold(0.0, |a, s| (s - m).mul_add(s - m, a))`, compare `start_fix` bitwise against a reference fold for random 15-sample windows. Add a hand-built window where a sample sits exactly at 3σ under the fused sum.

6. **The pose round trip to charge_manager's f32 inputs.**
   - Vendor (0x1603d0 + charge_manager lambda #1 0x17d530): x + 0.7599999904632568 and quaternion(yaw) on the wire. charge_manager takes `(float)x`, `(float)y` and `quaternionToEuler(q)` as yaw.
   - Ours: D:309 `from_robot_in_dock` → PoseStamped quaternion (M:155) → atan2 decode (C:429) → `robot_in_dock` (K:263) → `- pose_offset_x` → filter → `+ pose_offset_x as f32` (K:1049). quaternionToEuler is never emulated, and the x/y round trip is only < 1e-12 (D:541-556), not bit-exact.
   - Test: property test over random vendor (x, y, yaw) asserting `Docking`'s TargetPose equals `(f32(x + 0.76), f32(y), f32(quaternionToEuler(sincos(yaw/2))))` bitwise. Port quaternionToEuler from cm_decomp.c:1613 for the reference.

7. **RadiusOutlierRemoval's dense path (unverified).**
   - Vendor (PCL 1.10 libpcl_filters, dense input): keep iff 16-NN finds 16 points and d²[15] <= (double)0.3f².
   - Ours (P:324): keep iff more than 15 points have d² < (f32)(0.3f as f64)².
   - Test: first confirm in libpcl_filters 1.10's disassembly (`RadiusOutlierRemoval<PointXYZ>::applyFilterIndices`) that the dense branch exists. Then add a cloud whose 16th neighbour lies exactly at d² = f32(0.09000000357627869) and assert keep or drop as PCL decides (tools/ harness against the real libpcl).

8. **No vendor topics and no service.**
